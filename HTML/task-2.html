Phase 1: HTML Fundamentals

Welcome to the first phase of web development! HTML acts as the architect of the web, structuring the bones of every site you visit.

Understanding Tags

Tags are the building blocks. A crucial tip for beginners: always remember to close your tags unless they are self-closing!

Text Formatting

Sometimes you need to underline a specific term to make it stand out. You might also need special formatting for technical writing. For example, writing a formula like H2O requires a subscript, while writing 102 requires a superscript.

The Golden Rule

Don't overcomplicate this phase. You just need to know how to structure the document properly.
